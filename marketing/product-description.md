## Medium Version (Product Page)

**What is AeroCool?**

AeroCool is a 3D-printed window insert that cools your room using nothing but wind and water. It sits in your window like a traditional AC unit, but it has no compressor, no fan, no electricity — and no electricity bill.

**How does it work?**

1. **Wind catches** on the outside of the tower
2. **Venturi funnels** accelerate the air, dropping its temperature
3. **Evaporative channels** add water cooling (like a swamp cooler, but smarter)
4. **Chimney effect** pulls hot air out the top, creating continuous airflow

**The result:** Cooler air flowing into your room, 24/7, for free.

**Why AeroCool?**

- **Zero electricity** — saves $50–80/month in summer
- **3D printed** — made locally, shipped locally, no overseas factories
- **Repairable forever** — every part replaceable for $15–30
- **Power outage proof** — works when the grid fails
- **Eco-friendly** — no refrigerant, no toxic waste, recyclable PETG

**Two models:**

- **AeroCool Flat** ($45–55) — thin panel for any window type
- **AeroCool Tower** ($110) — maximum cooling for double-hung windows
