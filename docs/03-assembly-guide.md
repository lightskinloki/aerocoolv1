# 03 — Assembly Guide

## AeroCool Tower Assembly

### Time Required
- **Experienced maker:** 45–60 minutes
- **First-time builder:** 90–120 minutes

### Tools Required
- None (fully snap-fit design)
- Optional: rubber mallet for tight joints
- Optional: silicone lubricant for water system seals

---

## Step-by-Step Assembly

### Step 1: Prepare All Parts

Lay out all 18 modules in order:

```
[Top Cap] → [Stage 1] → [Stage 2] → [Stage 3] → [Stage 4] → [Stage 5] → [Stage 6] → [Base Frame]
         ↑          ↑          ↑          ↑          ↑          ↑
    [Water System connects to Stage 1 inlet]
```

**Check:** All dovetail joints are clean, no print artifacts blocking connection.

### Step 2: Connect Stages 1–3

1. Align Stage 1 dovetail male connectors with Stage 2 female receivers
2. Press firmly until click (audible snap-fit confirmation)
3. Repeat for Stage 2 → Stage 3
4. Verify alignment: all funnels should form continuous vertical channels

**Tip:** If joints are too tight, gently sand the male connector with 220-grit sandpaper.

### Step 3: Connect Stages 4–6

1. Repeat snap-fit process for Stages 4, 5, and 6
2. Connect Stage 3 → Stage 4
3. Verify: 6 stages form a continuous tower, approximately 300mm (12") tall

### Step 4: Install Water Distribution System

1. Locate the water distribution module (thin channels, reservoir at top)
2. Snap-fit into Stage 1 inlet zone
3. Verify: water channels align with Stage 1 funnel inlets
4. Fill reservoir with 200ml water
5. Check for leaks: water should flow through channels, not drip from joints

**Note:** First fill may take 30 seconds for water to saturate channels. This is normal.

### Step 5: Attach Top Cap

1. Snap-fit top cap onto Stage 1 (top of tower)
2. Verify: exhaust vents align with chimney opening
3. Check: hot air can escape freely upward

### Step 6: Attach Base Frame

1. Snap-fit base frame onto Stage 6 (bottom of tower)
2. Verify: base is level, all feet contact surface evenly
3. Check: mounting bracket attachment points are accessible

### Step 7: Install Side Panels

1. Attach 4 side panels to tower exterior
2. Panels snap into keyed slots on base frame and top cap
3. Verify: panels block light and debris, do not obstruct airflow

### Step 8: Install Mounting Bracket

1. Attach metal mounting bracket to base frame (2 screws, pre-threaded holes)
2. Bracket extends 50mm (2") into room for stability
3. Bracket hooks over window sill for anti-tip security

**Critical:** Bracket must be installed for safe operation. Tower will tip without it.

### Step 9: Window Installation

1. Open double-hung window to 12–18" (tower height)
2. Place tower in window opening, bracket resting on sill
3. Compress foam gasket between tower and window frame
4. Close window sash onto top of tower
5. Lock window or install screw-in security bracket
6. Verify: tower is stable, cannot be pushed inward or outward

### Step 10: First Operation

1. Fill water reservoir to 200ml mark
2. Wait 5 minutes for water to saturate channels
3. Check airflow: hold hand near outlet — you should feel cool air movement
4. Measure temperature: thermometer 30cm (12") from outlet
5. Compare to outside temperature: expect 5–15°C (9–27°F) drop

**Note:** Performance depends on wind speed and humidity. See [Performance Data](../data/performance-metrics.csv) for expected values.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| No airflow | Window not open enough | Open window fully, ensure exhaust path clear |
| Weak airflow | Low wind + no stack effect | Increase tower height (add stages) or use fan assist |
| Water leaks | Channels not saturated | Wait 10 minutes, refill reservoir |
| Algae growth | Standing water | Add 1 drop bleach per liter, or use distilled water |
| PETG warping | Heat exposure >60°C | Move tower to shaded window, or use ASA/ABS for hot climates |
| Tower tipping | Bracket not installed | Install mounting bracket immediately |

---

## Disassembly for Repair

All joints are reversible snap-fit:

1. Apply gentle pressure to release tabs on dovetail joints
2. Separate stages in reverse order (6 → 1)
3. Replace damaged module with new print
4. Reassemble

**Tip:** Photograph assembly before disassembly to ensure correct reassembly.
