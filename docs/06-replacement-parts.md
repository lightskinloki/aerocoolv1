> Repair should be cheap, fast, and accessible. But not so cheap that it undermines the value of the assembled unit.

## Ordering Process

### For Verified Owners

1. Log into [aerocool.io/repair](https://aerocool.io/repair) (coming soon)
2. Enter serial number from your base frame bracket
3. System verifies ownership in registry
4. Select needed part(s)
5. Order routes to nearest certified maker
6. Maker prints, packs, and ships within 48 hours
7. Tracking number provided automatically

### For Non-Owners

- Purchase blocked at checkout
- Redirected to assembled unit ($110) or free files ($0)
- Cannot buy rebuild kit without verified ownership

## The $15 Floor

$15 is the minimum viable price for any replacement part. Below this:

- Maker hourly drops below $6/hr (not worth the time)
- Shipping reimbursement becomes disproportionate
- Customer perceives part as "cheap/flimsy" rather than "quality repair"

The $15 floor applies even to simple parts (side panels) because:
- Packaging and shipping have fixed costs
- Maker time has minimum value
- Brand positioning requires consistency

---

## The $30 Ceiling

$30 is the maximum for individual parts. Above this:

- Customers consider buying a new unit instead
- "Repair vs. replace" math favors replacement
- Maker profit margin becomes excessive relative to value

The $30 ceiling applies to complex parts (base frame, complete stage sets) because:
- These are rare failures (once in 10+ years)
- High maker profit is justified by low frequency
- Still cheaper than $110 new unit

---

## DIY Alternative

Every part is 3D printable. Verified owners can:

1. Download free STL files from GitHub
2. Print part themselves (~$1–4 in filament)
3. Install in 15 minutes

**Why buy from a maker instead?**
- Convenience (no printer, no time)
- Quality assurance (certified maker, tested part)
- Warranty (defective part replaced free)
- Supporting the maker network
