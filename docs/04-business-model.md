# 04 — Business Model

## Philosophy

> We are a mission-driven company. Makers are our partners. We take 30% to cover insurance, platform costs, and R&D. Makers keep 70% plus we reimburse shipping and handle all customer service.

---

## Revenue Share

| Party | Share | Responsibility |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| **Maker** | 70% | Print, assemble, quality control, pack, ship |
| **Company** | 30% | IP protection, insurance, platform, marketing, customer service, R&D |

---

## Unit Economics

### Assembled Unit ($110)

| Line Item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Customer pays | $110.00 |
| Maker gets (70%) | $77.00 |
| Maker costs (material, electric, packaging) | $14.00 |
| **Maker net** | **$63.00** |

| Company gets (30%) | $33.00 |

| **Company net** | **$17.00** |

## The Three Tiers

| Tier | Price | What It Is | Who Buys |
|------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Free Files** | $0 | STL downloads | DIY enthusiasts, makers, tinkerers |
| **Assembled Unit** | $110 | Complete tower, tested, ready | Busy homeowners, non-makers |
| **Replacement Parts** | $15–30 | Individual modules | Existing owners (verified) |

---

## The Rebuild Kit

- **Price:** $85
- **Contents:** All 6 stages + water system + top cap
- **Restriction:** Registry-gated — verified owners only
- **Purpose:** Restoration, not new builds
- **Frame:** *"For AeroCool owners with units 5+ years old. Replace all cooling stages for like-new performance. Not for new builds — download our free files if you're building from scratch."*

---

## Anti-Clone Strategy

| Layer | Mechanism | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Registry-gated rebuilds | Verified owners only | Blocks 95% of abuse |
| Serialized brackets | Unique ID on every unit | Enables tracking + warranty |
| QR → Maker Network | Scanned bracket invites signup | Converts 2% of DIYers to makers |
| Community + brand | Trust prevents clones | Better than lawyers |

## The Honest Frame

> *"AeroCool is not an air conditioner replacement. It is a zero-power supplemental cooling system that makes hot rooms survivable. In dry climates with breeze: expect 15–25°F cooling. In humid climates with light wind: expect 5–15°F cooling. With no wind at all: expect 8–14°F cooling from stack effect alone. It will not make your room cold. It will make your room livable."*
