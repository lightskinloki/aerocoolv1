## Application Process

### Step 1: Apply Online

Submit application at [makers.aerocool.io](https://makers.aerocool.io) (coming soon) with:
- Printer model and age
- Photo of recent print (quality assessment)
- Location (zip code for regional coverage)
- Availability (hours per week you can dedicate)

### Step 2: Print Test Unit

We send you the finalized CAD files and print profiles. You print one complete tower at your own expense (~$22 in filament).

**Timeline:** 2 weeks to print, assemble, and ship to us.

### Step 3: Quality Audit

We inspect your test unit for:
- Dimensional accuracy (funnel throat must be 2.5mm ± 0.1mm)
- Structural integrity (no layer separation, no warping)
- Assembly quality (tight joints, no gaps, no leaks)
- Aesthetic finish (no stringing, clean surfaces)

**Pass/Fail:** If you pass, you are certified. If you fail, we provide feedback and you can retry once.

### Step 4: Certification

Receive:
- Certified Maker badge (digital + physical sticker)
- Access to order routing system
- Maker handbook (best practices, troubleshooting, customer communication)
- Direct Slack/Discord channel access

## Ongoing Responsibilities

### Per Order

1. **Print** parts according to official profile (no modifications without approval)
2. **Assemble** using provided instructions (snap-fit, test airflow)
3. **Quality control** visual inspection + airflow test
4. **Pack** in branded box with foam padding
5. **Ship** within 48 hours with tracking number
6. **Report** any issues or defects to company

### Per Month

- Minimum 2 units (to maintain active status)
- Average quality score >4.5/5 (from customer reviews)
- Response time to orders <24 hours

## FAQ

**Q: What if I don't have a printer?**
A: You cannot join as a maker. But you can buy an assembled unit or download free files and use a local library makerspace.

**Q: Can I use PLA instead of PETG?**
A: No. PLA warps at 55°C. PETG is required for heat tolerance. ASA is acceptable in very hot climates.

**Q: What if a customer returns a unit?**
A: Company handles returns. You keep your 50% shipping payment. If the return is due to print defect, we work with you to improve quality.

**Q: Can I sell AeroCool units on my own?**
A: No. Commercial sale requires certified maker status and use of our platform. This protects quality and brand integrity.

**Q: What if I want to quit?**
A: No penalty. Simply complete pending orders and deactivate your account. You keep all earnings.
