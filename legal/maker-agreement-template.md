## AeroCool Certified Maker Agreement

### Parties
- **Company:** [AeroCool LLC, address, contact]
- **Maker:** [Name, address, printer model, location]

### 1. Relationship
Maker is an independent contractor, not an employee. Maker operates their own business, sets their own hours, and provides their own equipment.

### 2. Certification
Maker has successfully completed the AeroCool quality audit and is authorized to produce:
- Assembled AeroCool Tower units
- Replacement parts for verified owners

### 3. Revenue Share
- Maker receives 70% of customer payment for assembled units
- Maker receives 70% of customer payment for replacement parts
- Company retains 30% for platform, insurance, marketing, and R&D
- Company reimburses shipping costs for all orders

### 4. Quality Standards
Maker agrees to:
- Use official CAD files and print profiles (no modifications)
- Maintain dimensional accuracy (±0.1mm on critical features)
- Perform visual inspection and airflow test on every unit
- Ship within 48 hours of order confirmation
- Maintain customer rating above 4.5/5 stars

### 5. Intellectual Property
- All CAD files, designs, and branding remain Company property
- Maker receives limited license to print for AeroCool customers only
- Maker may not sell AeroCool designs independently or through other platforms
- Maker may not share CAD files with non-certified parties

### 6. Liability
- Company maintains product liability insurance covering defects
- Maker is responsible for print quality and assembly accuracy
- Customer injuries caused by improper installation are not Maker's responsibility
- Maker must report any safety concerns immediately

### 7. Termination
- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice
- Maker must complete all pending orders before termination
- Company may terminate immediately for quality violations or IP breaches
- No penalty for voluntary termination

### 8. Dispute Resolution
- Disputes resolved through binding arbitration
- Arbitration in [state] under [arbitration association] rules
- Each party bears own legal costs
