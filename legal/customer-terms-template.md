## AeroCool Terms of Service

### 1. Product Description
AeroCool is a passive cooling window insert. It uses physics (Venturi effect, evaporative cooling, stack effect) to reduce indoor temperature. It does not use electricity. It is not an air conditioner replacement. Performance varies by wind speed, humidity, and installation quality.

### 2. Expected Performance
- Dry climates (20% RH): 15–25°F (8–14°C) temperature drop
- Moderate climates (40% RH): 10–20°F (5–11°C) temperature drop
- Humid climates (60% RH): 5–15°F (3–8°C) temperature drop
- No wind conditions: 8–14°F (4–8°C) temperature drop from stack effect alone

**Note:** These are estimates. Actual performance depends on environmental conditions. AeroCool will not make your room cold. It will make your room more comfortable.
