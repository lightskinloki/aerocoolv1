# Material Guide

## Recommended: PETG

| Property | Value | Why It Matters |
|----------|-------|----------------|
| Heat tolerance | 75°C (167°F) continuous | Handles hot air without warping |
| UV resistance | Good | Sun exposure through window |
| Water resistance | Excellent | Evaporative cooling system |
| Printability | Moderate | Requires dry filament, slow speeds |
| Cost | $20-25/kg | Affordable for 1kg spool |
| Recyclability | Yes (recycling code #1) | Eco-friendly end-of-life |

## Print Settings for PETG

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Nozzle temperature | 230–250°C | Start at 240°C, adjust for flow |
| Bed temperature | 70–80°C | 75°C is sweet spot |
| Print speed | 40–60 mm/s | Slower than PLA for quality |
| Fan speed | 30–50% | Too much cooling causes layer separation |
| Retraction | 1.5mm @ 25mm/s | Longer than PLA to prevent stringing |
| Bed adhesion | Brim (8mm) | PETG warps; brim prevents corners lifting |
| Enclosure | Optional | Helps with large prints, prevents drafts |
| Filament dryness | CRITICAL | Wet PETG = bubbles, poor layer adhesion |

## Alternative Materials

| Material | Pros | Cons | Use Case |
|----------|------|------|----------|
| **ASA** | UV resistant, heat tolerant (100°C) | More expensive, harder to print | Very hot climates (Arizona, Nevada) |
| **ABS** | Heat tolerant (90°C), strong | Warping, fumes, needs enclosure | Hot climates, experienced makers |
| **PLA+** | Easy to print, cheap | Warps at 55°C, degrades in UV | NOT RECOMMENDED for AeroCool |
| **Nylon** | Extremely tough, heat tolerant | Expensive, absorbs moisture | High-stress parts only |
| **TPU** | Flexible, impact resistant | Not rigid enough | Gaskets, seals only |

## Color Recommendations

| Part | Recommended Color | Why |
|------|-------------------|-----|
| Funnel arrays | White or light gray | Reflects heat, reduces solar absorption |
| Base frame | Black or dark gray | Hides dirt, UV-stable pigments |
| Water system | Blue or transparent | Visual water level indicator |
| Side panels | Any | Aesthetic preference |
| Top cap | White | Reflects heat from exhaust |

## Storage

- Keep filament in airtight bag with desiccant
- Dry at 65°C for 4 hours if exposed to humidity
- PETG absorbs moisture from air — print quality degrades visibly
