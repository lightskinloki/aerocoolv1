## What Is Patented

The patent covers the **specific combination** of:
1. Multi-stage Venturi funnel arrays in a vertical stack configuration
2. Integrated evaporative water channels between expansion zones
3. Stack-effect chimney design for passive airflow without external wind
4. Modular, snap-fit, 3D-printable architecture

## What Is NOT Patented (and remains open source)

- Individual Venturi funnel geometry (prior art, public domain)
- Evaporative cooling concept (prior art, thousands of years old)
- 3D printing as a manufacturing method (public domain)
- General passive cooling principles (public domain)

## Why Patent + Open Source?

The patent protects the **commercial ecosystem**:
- Prevents Amazon clone factories from mass-producing identical units
- Protects certified makers from unfair competition
- Ensures quality control through the maker network

The open source releases the **design**:
- Individuals can print for personal use without restriction
- Community can improve and remix designs
- Academic research can build on our work
- Developing regions can access cooling technology freely

## Patent Strategy

1. **Provisional patent** — establishes priority date, low cost ($75–150)
2. **12-month development** — refine design, gather data, build market presence
3. **Full patent filing** — if product gains traction, invest $5,000–15,000
4. **International patents** — PCT filing for EU, UK, Canada, Australia
5. **Defensive publication** — publish non-critical improvements to prevent competitors from patenting around us

## Maker Network Protection

The patent explicitly licenses certified makers to produce and sell units. Makers are not infringing by operating within the network.
